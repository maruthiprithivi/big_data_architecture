    Supported thresholds: 1, 10, 20, 30 or 40 minutes. The model proposes a bounded plan; the app validates it before executing SQL.

    What’s in the data? What can’t it prove?

    1,000,000 synthetic sessions. One row is one session, not one person. Each watch time is either 1 or 30 minutes; move the threshold and test what changes. Event timestamps span ten fixed days; this is a batch dataset.

    No random assignment, equal exposure, satisfaction or revenue measurements. A changed ranking is evidence about a measure, not proof of business uplift. The proposed next experiment is not a measured gain.
